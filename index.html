Guidelines On How to Use the Website

How to Report a Lost Item?

Mobile Users: Tap the Hamburger Icon Menu and select "Report Item".

Desktop/Laptop Users: Click the "Report Item" in the navigation bar.

How to Search for Found Items?

Mobile Users: Tap the Hamburger Icon Menu and select "Category Page" and select from the available categories of your choice and tap "view" to see the image's photo..

Desktop/Laptop Users: Click the "Category Page" in the navigation bar bar and select from the available categories of your choice and tap "view" to see the image's photo.

How to Claim an Item?

Mobile Users: Tap the Hamburger Icon Menu and select "Claim It here" button once you view the item from the "Category Page" then fill up the required details.

Desktop/Laptop Users: Click the "Chatbox" in the navigation bar and select "Claim It here" button once you view the item from the "Category Page" then fill up the required details.
